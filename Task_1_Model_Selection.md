# LegalEase – AI-Powered Legal Document Generator

## Activity 1.1: Research and Select the Appropriate Generative AI Model

### Project Requirements

LegalEase generates structured legal documents such as contracts, NDAs, and agreements based on:

- Document type
- Parties involved
- Terms and conditions
- Effective dates

### Selected Generative AI Model

Google Gemini 1.5 Pro

### Reasons for Selection

- Long context window of up to 1 million tokens
- Supports structured prompting with multi-part content
- Fast generation speed
- Handles complex formatting, reasoning, and structured text generation
- Generates formal documents with clear sections and legal terminology
- Dynamically generates clauses based on user inputs

### Application

Gemini 1.5 Pro receives the document type, parties, terms, and effective date from LegalEase and generates the required legal document content.

### Final Selection

Gemini 1.5 Pro is selected as the Generative AI model for LegalEase.
